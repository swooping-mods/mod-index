# Moon Sweepers mods

## Gameplay

- [MarsSweepers](https://github.com/Elchi-dev/MarsSweepers) — Mars, dust storms and new machinery.
- [Building Drone](https://github.com/swooping-mods/building-drone) — Buy the $2000 drone in Modded Shop after Gravity Boots; equip its controller.
- [Bumper Cars](https://github.com/swooping-mods/bumper-cars) — Alt+B equips an arcade bumper kit on the current car.
- [Dinosaur Bone Discoveries](https://github.com/swooping-mods/dinosaur-bones) — Find four fossil sites, including the supplied textured raptor skull.
- [Flag Finder](https://github.com/swooping-mods/flag-finder) — Buy the $125 radar in Modded Shop; equip it in the native hotbar.
- [Futuristic Courier](https://github.com/swooping-mods/futuristic-car) — Independent flying Courier using shared hover physics.
- [Credits Gambling](https://github.com/swooping-mods/gambling) — Ctrl+G wagers a small amount of virtual game credits.
- [Handheld Vacuum](https://github.com/swooping-mods/handheld-vacuum) — Buy the Handheld Vacuum in Modded Shop, then assign it in Equipment.
- [J.O.O.N.A.S. Backpack](https://github.com/swooping-mods/joonas-backpack) — Buy the backpack in Modded Shop after its late-game upgrade requirement.
- [Little Helpers](https://github.com/swooping-mods/little-helpers) — E buys at the wall terminal, upgrades capacity or greets a nearby plush helper.
- [Power Poles](https://github.com/swooping-mods/manual-power-poles) — Automatic passive power relays in the normal Build menu.
- [Modded Shop](https://github.com/swooping-mods/modded-shop) — Separate native Modded Shop with real registered item purchases.
- [Multi Belt Balancer](https://github.com/swooping-mods/multi-belt-balancer) — Configure 2x2, 3x3 and 4x4 native belt junctions; aligned 3x3 layouts display the supplied housing.
- [Player Catapult](https://github.com/swooping-mods/player-catapult) — Buy the Player Catapult in Modded Shop and assign its Equipment slot.
- [Portable Map](https://github.com/swooping-mods/portable-map) — Buy the $200 map in Modded Shop; equip it in the native hotbar.
- [Rover Horns](https://github.com/swooping-mods/rover-horns) — Driver horn playback prepared for operator-supplied Omni WAV audio.
- [Rustbound Vessel](https://github.com/swooping-mods/rustbound-vessel) — New fast enclosed hover vessel, powered by Vehicle Framework.
- [Slime Pet](https://github.com/swooping-mods/slime-pet) — Slime companion with hops, pickup cuddles and soft positional sounds.
- [Squishy Plushie](https://github.com/swooping-mods/squishy-plushie) — A free starter-base plush: pick up, throw, bounce and squeeze.
- [Sticker Label Gun](https://github.com/swooping-mods/sticker-label-gun) — Buy the Sticker Label Gun in Modded Shop, then assign it in Equipment.
- [YouTube Music](https://github.com/swooping-mods/youtube-music) — Host-controlled YouTube music with guest catch-up and local volume.

## Quality of life

- [Area Blueprints](https://github.com/swooping-mods/area-blueprints) — Save five area layouts in selection flight; rebuild standard structures with vanilla placement checks and costs.
- [Compass Cardinals](https://github.com/swooping-mods/compass-cardinals) — Buy the $100 passive compass upgrade in Modded Shop.
- [Conveyor Upgrade Wrench](https://github.com/swooping-mods/conveyor-upgrade-wrench) — Buy Conveyor Engineering in Modded Shop (default $125).
- [Custom Keybinds](https://github.com/swooping-mods/custom-keybinds) — Configure 26 vanilla action bindings; Ctrl+Home reloads.
- [Debug Menu](https://github.com/swooping-mods/debug-menu) — F4 opens in-game test controls, movement tools and diagnostics.
- [Edge Cruise Upgrade](https://github.com/swooping-mods/edge-cruise) — Purchased car upgrade follows cleared dust edges during cruise.
- [Efficient Robots](https://github.com/swooping-mods/efficient-robots) — Optional native vacuum and Joonas crowd collection optimization.
- [Electricity Lines](https://github.com/swooping-mods/electricity-toggle) — See nearby blue power connections. F8 hides or restores them.
- [Garage Management](https://github.com/swooping-mods/garage-management) — Select exact owned vehicles and confirm upgrades by storage identity.
- [Hud Marker Controls](https://github.com/swooping-mods/hud-marker-controls) — Map controls for service HUD marker types, range and distance labels.
- [Idle Vehicle Storage](https://github.com/swooping-mods/idle-vehicle-storage) — Automatically recalls empty idle owned vehicles after a configurable timeout.
- [Non-dust Capsule Stacking](https://github.com/swooping-mods/item-stacking) — Ctrl+K stacks nearby matching Sand or Stone capsules.
- [J.O.O.N.A.S. Flag Mapping](https://github.com/swooping-mods/joonas-flag-mapping) — Nearby robot discoveries persist as amber markers on the map globe.
- [J.O.O.N.A.S. Map Commands](https://github.com/swooping-mods/joonas-map-commands) — Shared map controls: select robots, move, assign sectors, stop, resume and roam.
- [Multiple Owned Vehicles](https://github.com/swooping-mods/multiple-vehicles) — Alt+N buys another selected garage model at its normal native price.
- [Pickup Hands](https://github.com/swooping-mods/pickup-hands) — Automatically frees your hands when picking up coffee, capsules or supported toys.
- [Precision Build Snapping](https://github.com/swooping-mods/precise-build-snapping) — F9 toggles snapping; enabled snapping reaches 30 cm for belts and machines.
- [Satellite GPS](https://github.com/swooping-mods/satellite-gps) — Straight-line driven GPS with purchased autonomous car modules and map commands.
- [Sell Cars](https://github.com/swooping-mods/sell-cars) — Alt+Delete twice sells an empty stopped owned car at half offer price.
- [Shared Vehicle Access](https://github.com/swooping-mods/shared-vehicle-access) — Alt+F borrows an aimed unoccupied vehicle through native holder handover.
- [Shift Multi-Build](https://github.com/swooping-mods/shift-multi-build) — Hold Shift after successful machine placement to keep the selected building ghost.
- [Vehicle First Person](https://github.com/swooping-mods/vehicle-first-person) — Alt+V toggles the vanilla seated first-person camera.
- [Vehicle Shop Statistics](https://github.com/swooping-mods/vehicle-shop-stats) — Compare vehicles in a separate specifications grid without changing descriptions.
- [MOZA Vehicle Controls](https://github.com/swooping-mods/wheel-control) — R9 / KS Pro steering and separate Simsonn Pro pedals for vehicles.
- [Zoom Module](https://github.com/swooping-mods/zoom-module) — Buy the Zoom Module in Modded Shop, then assign it in Equipment.

## Frameworks

- [Build Menu Framework](https://github.com/swooping-mods/build-menu-framework) — Register modded items in the normal Build menu.
- [Equipment Framework](https://github.com/swooping-mods/equipment-framework) — Register modded tools in the normal inventory and handheld quickslots.
- [Hints Framework](https://github.com/swooping-mods/hints-framework) — Shared native bottom control hints for equipped modded items.
- [Map Control Framework](https://github.com/swooping-mods/map-control-framework) — Shared native map top controls for robots and vehicle GPS.
- [Shop Framework](https://github.com/swooping-mods/shop-framework) — Separate native Modded Shop with real registered item purchases.
- [Sound Framework](https://github.com/swooping-mods/sound-framework) — Reusable spatial sound cues for modded items and companions.
- [Moving Unit Runtime](https://github.com/swooping-mods/unit-runtime) — Shared clocks, spatial queries and host-authoritative unit sessions.
- [Vehicle Control Framework](https://github.com/swooping-mods/vehicle-control-framework) — Shared wheel, GPS and Edge Cruise input with per-car assistance ownership.
- [Vehicle Framework](https://github.com/swooping-mods/vehicle-framework) — Shared foundation for additional vehicles.
